Alphacoeur Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

In short

Alphacoeur is an assistant that runs on your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac). We, the publisher of Alphacoeur, collect no data: no account, no Alphacoeur server, no advertising, no tracking, no usage statistics, no crash reports sent by the app. The only things that reach us are the emails you choose to write to us (see “Reporting an answer”).

When an answer needs the Internet, Alphacoeur contacts a public service directly (weather, search…), as described below. That service receives the request, just as if you had typed it into a browser yourself, but no identifier and no personal data coming from Alphacoeur.

Who is responsible

Publisher: Barth. Contact: barth.contact.alpha@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your notes, shopping list, projects, memory, scenarios, missions, skills, measurements and 3D scans, the action log and your conversations with the local brain.
  • Speech recognition (Alphacoeur requires on-device recognition: your voice is never sent) and the voice that answers you.
  • The AI brain, once downloaded: it runs entirely on the device.
  • The index of your photos and of the file folder you choose, if you turn it on. It is built on the device; card numbers, IBANs and codes are not copied into it. Photos and files kept only in iCloud are not downloaded.
  • Your calendar and contacts: read on the device, only when you ask. Your calendar is never sent, neither to a search engine nor to an online AI.
  • The online AI keys you enter: stored in the device keychain, never backed up or transferred.

Alphacoeur's main files are protected by the system's encryption (iOS / iPadOS, or FileVault on Mac) while the device is locked. Everything can be erased from Alphacoeur's Settings or by deleting the app.

Device backups: as with any app, Alphacoeur's data is part of your device's iCloud or computer backup if you have turned it on (the AI brain is excluded). These backups are managed by Apple or by your computer, not by Alphacoeur.

Alphacoeur backup: if you create a backup from Alphacoeur's Settings, it is a file encrypted with your password (AES-256), which you save wherever you like. Alphacoeur sends it nowhere. Without your password, no one can read it, not even Alphacoeur.

Permissions

Microphone, speech recognition, photos, calendar, contacts, camera (measure and 3D scan), local network, notifications and alarms are used only when you ask for the matching feature. You can withdraw them at any time in the device's Settings.

  • Wake word (off by default): if you turn it on, the microphone also listens while Alphacoeur is on screen, to recognize “Alpha”. Everything happens on the device, nothing is recorded, and the microphone closes as soon as Alphacoeur goes to the background.
  • Notifications, reminders and alarms: scheduled on the device (local notifications and, on recent iPhones and iPads, system alarms). No notification comes from a server.
  • Camera and LiDAR: only in “Measure & 3D scan”; images and scans stay on the device.
  • Photos: read-only, analyzed on the device; Alphacoeur never modifies or deletes any photo.
  • Messages and emails: Alphacoeur prepares a draft; it is sent only if you confirm sending, in Messages or Mail.
  • Clipboard: read only when you ask to use the copied text or link.

Online services

When an answer needs the Internet, Alphacoeur queries the relevant service directly, with no identifier and no personal data, sending only what is needed (for example the name of a city). As with any Internet connection, these services see your connection's IP address. Each one applies its own privacy policy.

  • places and time zones: Apple geocoding (the city name; the city you set is kept on the device after the first time);
  • weather: MET Norway, the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (api.met.no), which only receives the city's position rounded to about 1 km; sunrise and sunset are calculated on the device, without the network;
  • exchange rates: Frankfurter, based on the European Central Bank (the codes of the two currencies, never the amount);
  • searches: Wikipedia (the search words, cleaned of anything about you). Alphacoeur then opens a few Wikipedia articles to read the answer; no other site is opened;
  • images: Wikimedia Commons (the search words); only free images are shown, with their author and license;
  • summary of a link you provide: the page is opened from your device and summarized on the device;
  • links you tap in Alphacoeur (sources, requested sites): they open in Safari, outside Alphacoeur, and the site sees your visit like any other visit in Safari;
  • AI brain download: Hugging Face (only when you start the installation, over Wi‑Fi);
  • directions: Apple Maps (only if you open the link);
  • connection test: an Apple test page, only when you ask for the device status.

What is sent without you saying “Search”:

  • factual questions (“What is…”, “How many…”) may be checked automatically on the web. This setting, “Check questions on the web” (on by default), can be turned off in Alphacoeur's Settings;
  • scenarios with a weather condition query the weather (Apple geocoding, then MET Norway) when they run;
  • requests made without a network are kept on the device, then sent in the same way when the connection comes back.

What is never sent on its own: a sentence about you or your loved ones (“my daughter has a fever”, an address, a code…) is never sent automatically. And even when you say “Search…”, Alphacoeur removes from the search family relationships, people's first and last names, addresses and possessives (“the treatment of my mother Josiane's cancer” becomes “the treatment of cancer”).

Online AI (optional): if you enter your own Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Gemini (Google) key, the question concerned is sent directly to that provider, with your key, and handled under its own terms. Alphacoeur asks for your consent before saving the key. Your loved ones' names, your address and numbers (phone, email, IBAN, card) are removed from the question or block the sending. This only happens if you ask for it (“ask Claude…”) or if you turn on “Automatically send difficult questions” (off by default; a sentence about you is then never sent). Neither your memory nor your notes, calendar, photos or files are sent. Without a key, Alphacoeur may show an “Go deeper on ChatGPT” or “Open Claude” link: the question is only sent if you tap it (this link can be turned off in Settings).

Siri: if you go through “Hey Siri, ask Alphacoeur”, the request is first handled by Siri, under Apple's policy.

Reporting an answer: on an answer written by an AI, “Report this answer” prepares an email addressed to the publisher, containing only your question, the answer, its origin (local brain or online AI) and the app version. Nothing is sent automatically: you review the email and decide whether to send it from your mail app. If you send it, the publisher receives this email and your email address, used only to handle the report and reply to you, then deleted.

Subscription and payment

Alphacoeur is free to download; the optional “Alphacoeur Premium” subscription is purchased in the app with your Apple Account. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: we receive no payment data (no bank card, name, address or Apple ID). Apple processes the purchase under its own privacy policy.

Alphacoeur checks the subscription on your device, in receipts signed by Apple, without any Alphacoeur server: nothing is sent to the publisher. You manage and cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings. Emergency, health and privacy features (erasure, privacy settings, backup and restore) always remain free.

Transfers between your devices

Files exchanged between your Alphacoeur devices go directly from one to the other, on the same Wi‑Fi network, end-to-end encrypted, with no online service, and only while the Transfers screen is open. A device is only authorized after comparing a 6-digit code. Your memory is only shared with a device you have authorized for it, when you start the synchronization.

Health and emergencies

Alphacoeur can give general health pointers and display emergency numbers (15, 112, 114, 3114, 3919, 119…). These numbers are simply displayed or read aloud: Alphacoeur calls no one and sends nothing to these services. Your health questions are handled on the device and are never sent to the Internet on their own. Alphacoeur does not replace a doctor, a pharmacist or emergency services.

Your rights

Since we receive no data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase on our side: you stay in control of everything, from Alphacoeur (view, export, erase) or by deleting the app. For data sent to an online service listed above, contact that service. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).

Children

Alphacoeur collects no data, including from children.
